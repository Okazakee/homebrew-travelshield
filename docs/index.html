Why TravelShield?

TPM2 auto-unlock is convenient at home: you power on and the disk decrypts silently. But when you travel, that same convenience becomes a liability.

Crossing a border? Attending a conference? Leaving your laptop in a hotel room? With the TPM slot active, anyone who powers on your machine gets straight to the desktop. TravelShield lets you temporarily disable TPM auto-unlock so only your LUKS passphrase can decrypt the disk. When you're back in a safe place, re-enable it with the same tool. (Non-TPM automatic unlockers — e.g. Clevis tang — are reported but never removed; if one is configured, TravelShield does not claim "passphrase required".)

Background reading: Unlocking LUKS2 volumes with TPM2, FIDO2, PKCS#11 - 0pointer

Travel Mode

[ARMED] - ON

  • Verified: no supported TPM2 auto-unlock binding remains
  • Passphrase required at boot (unless a non-TPM unlocker is reported)
  • Safe for border crossings, transit, shared spaces

[DISARMED] - OFF

  • TPM binding enrolled and sealed to PCR7 (sha256)
  • Auto-unlock via TPM2
  • Convenient at home or in a trusted environment

STALE = binding present but PCR7 diagnostic mismatch (never "token absent"). UNKNOWN = header cannot be classified safely (no toggling, never ARMED). ERROR = last operation failed or was partial (residual state shown). ARMED is only reported after a fresh header inspection proves zero supported TPM bindings.

v2.0.2 and older enrolled Clevis with an empty {} policy — no PCR policy at all. If you enrolled with an old version, use [R] Re-enroll to replace the binding with a PCR7-sealed one. Recovery safety is structural: TravelShield refuses removal when no passphrase keyslot survives, requires a typed confirmation, and never claims your recovery material is verified. The stored PCR7 fingerprint is diagnostic only; PCR7 is tied to Secure Boot state and certificates, not to one unique boot image.

Technical Details

systemd-cryptenroll

Primary backend. Uses built-in systemd ≥ 248 support to enroll or wipe TPM2 tokens on LUKS2 headers.

clevis-luks

Fallback backend. Binds with an explicit PCR7 policy (sha256 bank, PCR 7) — never the insecure empty config.

PCR7 Diagnostics

Stores a SHA-256 digest of PCR7 per volume. Diagnostic only — it never authorizes state; a mismatch is STALE, never "token absent". PCR7 relates to Secure Boot state, not a unique boot image.

Initramfs Reminder

Auto-detects mkinitcpio, dracut, or update-initramfs and tells you exactly what to run before rebooting.

Distro-Agnostic Discovery

Locates your root LUKS device via lsblk, findmnt, and blkid - no hardcoded paths, works across Arch, Debian, Fedora, Void, Alpine, and more. Requires jq.

Single-Key TUI

No Enter key needed. Tap T, R, S, or Q. Colored status output gives instant clarity on system state.
